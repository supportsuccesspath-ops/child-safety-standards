CHILD SAFETY STANDARDS

Children have no place in sexual exploitation.

Praptham is an adults-only matrimonial service. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation and act on reports that may put a child at risk.

Effective September 26, 2026

Anyone under 18 must not create an account, use Praptham, or appear in sexualized content shared through the service. These standards apply to all members and all material or interactions available through Praptham.

01   Not allowed

Prohibited conduct

We do not allow users to create, request, share, promote, or facilitate child sexual abuse or exploitation. Prohibited conduct includes:

  • Child sexual abuse material or sexualized depictions of anyone under 18, including altered or computer-generated material presented as a real child.
  • Sexual solicitation, grooming, or attempts to move a child into sexual contact or exploitation.
  • Sextortion, coercion, blackmail, or threats involving a child or sexual material of a child.
  • Child sex trafficking, arranging or advertising the sexual exploitation of a child, or helping another person do so.
  • Sharing links, instructions, or other material intended to obtain, distribute, or conceal child sexual abuse or exploitation.
02   Make a report

Tell our safety team

Report suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation, an account used by someone under 18, or conduct that places a child at risk. Include the profile name or link, relevant dates, and a short description so we can locate and assess the issue.

Do not download, forward, attach, or otherwise redistribute suspected illegal images or videos. A profile or message link and a description are enough to start a review.

Child safety contact

Email the Praptham Child Safety Team:

SupportSuccessPath@gmail.com Use the subject “Child Safety Report”. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement first.
03   Our response

Review and protective action

We review child-safety reports and take action appropriate to the information available. This may include removing material, restricting or closing accounts, preserving relevant records, and reporting suspected abuse to competent authorities or child-protection organizations when required or appropriate under applicable law.

We cooperate with lawful investigations and take steps to prevent accounts or content removed for child-safety violations from returning to the service.

04   Adults only

Eligibility and underage accounts

Praptham is intended only for people who are 18 or older. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, or that a child is being exploited through the service, we may restrict the account and investigate. Reports can be sent to the child safety contact above.
